    About the science

    Mission Forge is an educational game, not a flight dynamics simulator. Equipment specifications, budgets, thresholds, and scoring are fictional gameplay values and should not be interpreted as NASA spacecraft specifications. The planetary context draws on NASA's publicly available science resources.
